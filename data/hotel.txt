Hotel Information

The Grand Ocean Hotel is a four-star hotel located in Lisbon.

Rooms

The hotel offers three room types:

Standard Room:
- Maximum occupancy: 2 guests
- One double bed
- Free Wi-Fi
- Air conditioning
- Private bathroom
- Breakfast is available for an additional fee

Deluxe Room:
- Maximum occupancy: 3 guests
- One king-size bed
- One sofa bed
- Free Wi-Fi
- Air conditioning
- Private bathroom
- Sea view
- Breakfast included

Family Suite:
- Maximum occupancy: 4 guests
- Two bedrooms
- One king-size bed
- Two single beds
- Free Wi-Fi
- Air conditioning
- Two private bathrooms
- Breakfast included

Facilities

The hotel provides:
- Outdoor swimming pool
- Fitness center
- Restaurant
- Bar
- 24-hour reception
- Free Wi-Fi
- Private parking

Check-in and Check-out

Check-in starts at 15:00.

Check-out is until 11:00.

The reception is open 24 hours a day.

Pets

Pets are allowed in selected rooms.

Guests should contact the hotel before arrival to confirm availability.

Cancellation

Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival.

Cancellations made less than 48 hours before arrival may be subject to a fee.
