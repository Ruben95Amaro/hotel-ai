Grand Ocean Hotel – Additional Information
Location
The Grand Ocean Hotel is situated in a beautiful coastal area of Lisbon, offering guests a relaxing atmosphere and stunning views of the Atlantic Ocean. The hotel is conveniently located near popular tourist attractions, shopping areas, restaurants and public transport.

Approximately 10 minutes from the city centre.

15 minutes from Lisbon's historic district.

20 minutes from Lisbon Airport.

Close to several beaches and seaside walking paths.

Easy access to bus, tram and metro stations.

Hotel Services
The hotel offers a variety of services to ensure a comfortable and enjoyable stay:

Daily housekeeping service.

Room service available from 07:00 to 23:00.

Laundry and dry-cleaning services.

Luggage storage before check-in and after check-out.

Airport transfer service upon request.

Car rental assistance.

Tourist information and guided tour bookings.

Free high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the hotel.

Restaurant and Dining
The Grand Ocean Restaurant serves a variety of Portuguese and international dishes, prepared with fresh and locally sourced ingredients.

Breakfast buffet served from 07:00 to 10:30.

Lunch served from 12:00 to 15:00.

Dinner served from 19:00 to 22:30.

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options available upon request.

Traditional Portuguese specialities, including fresh seafood and grilled fish.

The hotel bar offers a selection of cocktails, wines, soft drinks and light snacks. Guests can enjoy their drinks on the outdoor terrace overlooking the ocean.

Swimming Pool and Wellness
Guests can relax and enjoy the hotel's outdoor swimming pool, surrounded by sun loungers and comfortable seating areas.

Outdoor swimming pool open from 08:00 to 20:00.

Pool towels available free of charge.

Fitness centre open daily from 06:00 to 22:00.

Massage treatments available by appointment.

Sun terrace with ocean views.

Business Facilities
The hotel is also suitable for business travellers and professional events.

Two fully equipped meeting rooms.

Maximum meeting capacity of 50 people.

Free Wi-Fi and presentation equipment.

Printing and photocopying services.

Business support available at reception.

Additional Information
Check-in: From 15:00.

Check-out: Until 11:00.

Minimum age for check-in: 18 years.

Children: Children of all ages are welcome.

Extra beds: Available upon request, subject to availability.

Smoking policy: Smoking is prohibited inside the hotel.

Payment methods: Visa, Mastercard and cash.

Languages spoken: Portuguese, English, Spanish and French.

Parking: Private parking available for an additional daily fee.

Special Offers
The Grand Ocean Hotel offers several special packages throughout the year:

Romantic Getaway: Includes a bottle of sparkling wine, room decoration and breakfast in bed.

Family Package: Includes family accommodation and special activities for children.

Weekend Escape: Special discounted rates for weekend stays.

Long Stay Offer: Discounts for guests staying seven nights or more.

Hotel Sustainability
The Grand Ocean Hotel is committed to protecting the environment and promoting sustainable tourism.

Energy-efficient lighting throughout the building.

Recycling facilities available for guests.

Reduced use of single-use plastics.

Water-saving systems in bathrooms.

Preference for local suppliers and seasonal ingredients.
